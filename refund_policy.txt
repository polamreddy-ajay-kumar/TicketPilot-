Refund and Returns Policy

Customers may request a refund for an unused product within 30 calendar days of delivery. The customer must contact support with the order number to start a return. After the returned item is received and inspected, an approved refund is sent to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Original shipping charges are not refundable unless the item arrived defective or the wrong item was shipped. Final-sale items are not eligible for return or refund.
