Shipping and Delivery Policy

Orders require 1 to 2 business days of processing before dispatch. Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. A delivery date is an estimate, not a guarantee; weather, carrier disruptions, and customs may cause delays. Customers whose tracking has not updated or whose package has not arrived within 7 business days after dispatch should contact support with the order number. Tracking details are sent when the order is dispatched.
